About Us

Orange Blossom Mobile RV Solutions is a family-owned, mobile RV technician business that brings solutions to you. Open on nights and weekends, we service RVs generally within a 25-mile radius of Allison Park, PA. Service calls completed outside of this radius will incur an additional fee.

Due to the unpredictable nature of Cole’s status as an active duty servicemember, we have some, but limited capacity to be available on call.

Who We Are:

Cole – Technician, Owner

Cole is an active duty servicemember with over 17 years of experience that includes combat operations, special operations, maintenance, logistics, and several overseas deployments. As a Chief Warrant Officer 2 (P) on the cusp of retirement, Cole’s goal for the future is to build something meaningful for his family. A constant fiddler, he enjoys working with his hands and finding solutions for broken things. He has the knowledge, resourcefulness, and physical ability to help you get back on the road.

Courtnee – Not a Technician, Owner

Courtnee is Cole’s better half. A professional grant writer by day, Courtnee spends most of her nights and weekends wrangling her and Cole’s four young kids. She is an avid reader and good cook, but not mechanically inclined. She works behind the scenes, keeping Orange Blossom running smoothly. If she’s ever out on a service call, it’s just to learn something new (or bring cookies to a customer).
